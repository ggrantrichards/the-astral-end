The Starforge

Late-game upgrades for diamond and netherite gear. Each item takes one upgrade, and they work alongside enchantments.

  1. Defeat the CuratorIt drops two Starforge Templates. They don't come from anywhere else.
  2. Find the constellationEach upgrade has a constellation. Look at it through a spyglass while standing in the Gilded Reach.
  3. Build an Astral ForgeA Meteoric Iron block, five Observatory Quartz and a Void Crystal.
  4. Forge itPut in the template, the item, Starforge Alloy and the upgrade's material. Works on diamond and netherite gear, bows, crossbows, tridents and maces.
